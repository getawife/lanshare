# LANShare

**Send files instantly. No cloud. No USB. No setup.**

LANShare is a free, open-source desktop app that lets you transfer files and folders directly between computers on the same Wi-Fi or Ethernet network.

It works like magic: just open the app on both machines, pick a device, and drag-and-drop your files. Everything stays on your local network—nothing is ever uploaded to a third-party server.

> **🚀 Download now at [lanshare.app](https://lanshare.app/)**

---

## ✨ Features

- **Blazing fast transfers** – Use the full speed of your local network, no internet throttling.
- **No cloud required** – Your files stay on your own Wi-Fi or LAN. Zero data leaves your home or office.
- **Automatic discovery** – Nearby devices pop up instantly. No IP addresses or configuration needed.
- **Drag-and-drop ease** – Drag files or folders onto the app and send them in one click.
- **Live progress & integrity** – Watch the transfer in real-time. Every file is checksum-verified to ensure it arrived exactly as sent.
- **Folder structure preserved** – Send entire projects. Your folder trees remain perfectly intact on the receiving end.
- **Works offline** – No internet? No problem. As long as both computers are on the same network, you're good to go.
- **Cross-platform** – Available for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

---

## 🖱️ How to use it

1. **Download and install** LANShare from [lanshare.app](https://lanshare.app/).
2. **Open the app** on both computers (the sender and the receiver).
3. On the **sender** computer, click the device name you want to send to.
4. **Drag and drop** your files (or click "Select Files") into the drop zone.
5. Click **Send**.
6. On the **receiver** computer, click **Accept** when the prompt appears.

That's it. Your files will appear in your standard `Downloads` folder.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

LANShare is built with your privacy in mind:

- **All transfers are local.** Your files never touch the internet.
- **No account required.** We don't know who you are, and we don't track you.
- **Open-source code.** Anyone can audit exactly how the app works.
- **SHA-256 checksums.** Every file is verified bit-by-bit during transfer to prevent corruption.

> **Note:** While LANShare is safe on private home/business networks, we recommend caution when using it on public Wi-Fi (like coffee shops or airports), as other devices on the same network could technically see your connection.
